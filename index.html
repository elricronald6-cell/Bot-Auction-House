The Process
How a lot changes hands
I

Bidding

A lot is listed with a minimum bid and a fixed close time. Each bid is placed directly against the contract, and every bidder who is outbid is refunded the moment a higher offer lands — automatically, with nothing to claim.

II

Settlement

When the clock reaches zero, the auction is closed permanently. No extensions, no late offers, no discretion — the highest bid standing at the deadline wins the lot.

III

Escrow Release

The winning bid is released to the seller and the lot is recorded to the winner in a single transaction — funds never sit with a custodian at any point in the process.
